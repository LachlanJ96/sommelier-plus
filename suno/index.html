TAKEONE

Style prompts and lyric sheets for Suno v5 & v5.5

The brief

Type anything — it'll match to the closest profile.

A situation beats an abstraction. "My dad's ute broke down on the way to the coast" gives a better song than "loss".

Used as a description, never as a name. Suno blocks artist names and can strike an account for using one — this translates the reference into era, voice, instruments, mix and feel before it goes anywhere near the box.

Detail

Two or three at most. Past that the Exclude field stops steering.

Fill in the brief and hit Write it.

You'll get four things: a title, the Style field, the Exclude field, and a lyric sheet with the section tags already in place. Everything is built from the genre and artist tables in this folder — nothing is fetched, nothing is sent anywhere.
